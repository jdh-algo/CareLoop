# Friction-centered figure source manifest

Generated by `scripts/generate_cl120_friction_figures.py` from the canonical v0.2 friction tables and the v0.3 multidimensional Judge-agreement tables.

## Main-text figures

- `cl120_fig_friction_capability_heatmap.pdf`: six capability domains plus FCC for all 10 tested models.
- `cl120_fig_model_by_friction_heatmap.pdf`: FCC by tested model and prospectively authored primary-friction exposure.
- `cl120_fig_friction_judge_agreement.pdf`: ICC(A,1), ICC(A,4), pairwise trajectory correlations, and pairwise ten-model rank correlations for FCC, C-RWR, and six domains.
- `cl120_fig_added_resolution.pdf`: FCC distributions within frozen ordinal grades and model-rank comparison.

## Supplementary figures

- `cl120_sfig_friction_ho_coverage.pdf`: authored 10-friction × 8-HO coverage matrix.
- `cl120_sfig_judge_specific_capability.pdf`: Judge-specific six-domain capability profiles.

All performance heatmaps use a fixed 0–1 scale. The coverage matrix reports authored counts and is not a performance heatmap.
