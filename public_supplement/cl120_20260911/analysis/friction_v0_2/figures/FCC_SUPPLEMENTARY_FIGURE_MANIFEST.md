# FCC-primary Supplementary figure manifest

Figures included in the Supplementary Material:

1. `cl120_sfig_fcc_task_burden_associations.pdf`: five pre-execution burden dimensions versus FCC, C-RWR, and interaction length.
2. `cl120_sfig_fcc_matched_model_judge_association.pdf`: exploratory matched model–Judge association under FCC and C-RWR.
3. `cl120_sfig_fcc_model_diagnostic_map.pdf`: compact model-level FCC/C-RWR, safety-gap, Judge-dispersion, and operational profile.
4. `cl120_sfig_friction_ho_coverage.pdf`: authored HO opportunity coverage and triggered-evidence context.
5. `cl120_sfig_judge_specific_capability.pdf`: Judge-specific six-domain capability profiles.

The figure set is limited to views that clarify principal FCC/C-RWR findings, explain the CareLoop measurement layer, or identify questions for future validation. Threshold-dependent ordinal screening, dense score-ordered case maps, ordinal burden bins, standalone workspace-call frequency, and an aggregate-index scatterplot are not treated as substantive findings. Where useful for audit, the underlying continuous tables remain in the public analysis directory.
