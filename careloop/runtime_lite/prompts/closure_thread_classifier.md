# Closure Thread Classifier

Classify care-process closure threads semantically. This is advisory evidence for closure review, not a hard gate.

Consider presenting concern, diagnosis, active treatment, medication understanding, tests/results, receipt review, communication, feasibility/adherence, follow-up, safety-net, family alignment, and durable longitudinal management. Durable longitudinal management includes both stable follow-up and goal-directed noncurative management, but it must not be inferred from a mere mention of observation, referral, conservative/supportive/palliative care, quality of life, or patient refusal.

Return JSON: overall_closure_readiness, threads, main_open_threads, what_would_make_it_closed.
